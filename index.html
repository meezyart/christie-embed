The Story of Christie

DJ Christie is an electrifying force in the music scene, seamlessly blending electro, funk, and hip-hop into sets that move crowds from the first beat to the last drop. Known for genre-defying mixes and an infectious energy behind the decks, Christie has become a go-to name for events that demand nothing less than extraordinary.

From packed nightclubs to massive festivals, private events to corporate galas, every performance is a carefully crafted journey. With a deep love for music spanning decades of sound and a keen ear for what makes people move, DJ Christie delivers experiences that linger long after the music stops.
